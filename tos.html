Kalma wa Maana — Terms of Service

Kalma wa Maana is a short-form educational video brand that explains words and phrases of the Holy Quran in simple Egyptian Arabic.

Use of Service

Our app is used solely by the brand team to publish our own produced videos to our own social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook). No third-party users access the service.

Content

All videos are original productions based on verified Tafsir sources (Al-Tafsir Al-Muyassar, King Fahd Complex for the Printing of the Holy Quran). Quran recitations use recordings from Haramain Recordings (Saad Al-Ghamdi) distributed for public broadcasting.
